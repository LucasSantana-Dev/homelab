## Step 3: Check Results
**If Mushroom Cards is working, you should see:**
- ✅ **"🎉 Mushroom Cards Funcionando!"** title card
- ✅ **Clean, modern cards** with rounded corners
- ✅ **Beautiful icons** and colors
- ✅ **Professional layout**

**If it's not working, you'll see:**
- ❌ **"custom:mushroom-title-card"** text instead of the title
- ❌ **Same old dashboard** appearance

## Step 4: Clear Browser Cache
- **Hard refresh** the page (Ctrl+F5 or Cmd+Shift+R)
- Or try **incognito/private mode**

## Troubleshooting
- Make sure all resources are added
- Restart Home Assistant if needed
- Check browser console for errors
- Verify HACS components are installed
